What verification shows

A valid certificate record will show: certificate status, learner name, course name, certificate type, issue date, score (if applicable), certificate ID, and issuer name.

Email addresses and private contact details are not shown on the public verification page.

Certificate records and deletion

If a certificate holder requests deletion of their record, the certificate may no longer appear in verification results. This does not mean the certificate was invalid — the record may have been removed at the holder's request.
