About Solvanta Consulting

Solvanta Consulting focuses on practical professional learning without unnecessary complexity. Course content and practice exercises are being prepared in stages, and the site will stay lightweight until those resources are ready.

The goal is to provide useful explanations, realistic exercises, and support that respects privacy. Public course content does not require an account.

About Omkar

Hi, I'm Omkar.

Over the last eight years I've worked across operations, compliance, quality, training, automation, and process improvement. Most of my work has involved solving practical problems, simplifying complicated processes, and helping teams work more effectively.

I'm not a software engineer by background. Most of the tools and projects I build start with a simple question: "There has to be an easier way to do this."

Solvanta Consulting grew from that mindset. It's a place to share practical knowledge, real-world exercises, and lessons learned from building systems, improving workflows, and working with teams across different industries.

I hope the courses, resources, and tools here help make your work a little easier too.
