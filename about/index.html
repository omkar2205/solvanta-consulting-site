Purpose

Solvanta Learning provides free, practical learning resources for people who want to build everyday professional skills. The focus is on clear explanations, useful exercises, and certificates that can be verified online when issued.

What Solvanta Learning provides

The platform is designed around three main areas: courses, practice exercises, and certificate verification. Courses are being added in stages, starting with practical topics such as AI prompting, workplace communication, basic Excel, and interview readiness.

Privacy-first approach

Solvanta Learning collects only the information required for the feature being used. No account is required for public course content. Contact information is used only to respond to enquiries. Certificate records are held only to support verification requests.

Certificate position

Certificates issued by Solvanta Learning confirm completion of a Solvanta Learning course or assessment. They are not licences, government approvals, or accredited qualifications unless explicitly stated.
