Purpose

Solvanta Consulting provides free, practical learning resources for people who want to build everyday professional skills. The focus is on clear explanations, useful exercises, and certificates that can be verified online when issued.

What Solvanta Consulting provides

The platform is designed around three main areas: courses, practice exercises, and certificate verification. Courses are being added in stages, starting with practical topics such as AI prompting, workplace communication, basic Excel, and interview readiness.

Simple courses

Short learning resources focused on useful skills — no unnecessary complexity.

Practical exercises

Tasks designed to help you apply concepts in realistic everyday situations.

Verifiable certificates

Certificates include a unique ID and a public verification link anyone can check.

Privacy-first approach

Solvanta Consulting collects only the information required for the feature being used. No account is required for public course content. Contact information is used only to respond to enquiries. Certificate records are held only to support verification requests.

Certificate position

Certificates issued by Solvanta Consulting confirm completion of a Solvanta Consulting course or assessment. They are not licences, government approvals, or accredited qualifications unless explicitly stated.
